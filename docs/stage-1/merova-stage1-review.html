Compliance finding: 3 products held from publication

India's single-use plastic prohibition (in force since 1 July 2022) covers plastic "cups, glasses… trays" and "wrapping or packing films around sweet boxes". PET glasses, PP glasses and plastic trays are held until Merova confirms a lawful basis for supplying them (for example, export only). Cling film stays listed but must not be marketed for sweet boxes.

1 · Positioning

One accountable partner across paper, plastic, compostable and corrugated.

For manufacturers, food businesses, retailers and procurement teams that buy packaging in several materials, Merova specifies each pack around the product, prints it to brand standard and confirms it on a sample before production.

Proof pillars

  • One portfolio, four materials: visible in the navigation itself.
  • Specified, not sold: configurable parameters and product-specific RFQs.
  • Print as a discipline: proofing, finishes and registration.
  • Sample before production: the process page.
  • Material honesty: compostable, recyclable and conventional never blurred.

Headline system

  • Discovery: Every material has a purpose. Find the right one.
  • Paper: Make the first impression count.
  • Plastic & Films: Film that seals, wraps and protects at line speed.
  • Compostable: Compostable, stated precisely.
  • Corrugated: Strength that moves business forward.
  • Industries: Tell us what you run. We'll show you what you need.

Voice rules

Confident, exact, short. Every headline names a benefit a buyer can test.

Not used: "trusted partner", "one-stop", "best-in-class", "quality is our priority", or any figure Merova has not supplied.

Proposed additions

    Key deduplication decisions

      29 decisions in total, all in the Dedup Register tab.

      Boundary rules

      • Material decides the category, never the industry.
      • Certification decides compostable. PE-lined cups are Paper; certified cups are Compostable; the two are cross-linked.
      • Customisation is a journey, not four "custom" products.
      • Excluded: tissue and hygiene paper, stationery, books, diaries, household plastics, straws.

      5 · Website architecture

      Five menu items. Six journeys. One basket.

      Navigation

      • Products: four-column mega menu with category photography
      • Industries: industry rail and operator cards
      • About Merova: overview, manufacturing, quality, process, servicing, sustainability, leadership, careers, partnerships
      • Resources: downloads, technical resources, FAQs, insights
      • Contact
      • Plus search, quote basket and the red Request a Quote button

      URL scheme

      • /products/{category}/{product}/ with no subcategory segment
      • /industries/{industry}/{operator}/
      • 152 sitemap rows; 33 old URLs 301-redirected
      • Held and unapproved products stay out of the sitemap and are noindexed

      Journeys

      • A Product-first → add to enquiry
      • B Industry → operator → "add the complete kit"
      • C Describe a custom requirement and upload a photo
      • D Download a flyer → QR → product → quote
      • E Direct quote
      • F Mira recommends → adds to basket

      Conversion system

      • Multi-product quote basket that keeps variant and parameter context
      • Per-product RFQ fields drawn from the register, with progressive disclosure
      • Success shown only after the server confirms delivery
      • Leads routed by material mix to team inboxes or the CRM

      Mira

      • Claude via the Anthropic API, server-side
      • Tool use over the register only: search, operator kit, compare, downloads, add to enquiry, handover
      • No prices, lead times, certifications or unlisted specs
      • A 30-question evaluation set runs before launch

      SEO and collateral

      • One primary term per page; automated internal links; Product schema without prices
      • A one-page flyer per approved product (up to 68), 4 category brochures, master catalogue, company profile
      • Every PDF page is rasterised and inspected before release
